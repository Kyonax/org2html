  job-needs ordering, tolerant test step (passes on "no test files yet")

  reckit-style acronym tagline and shields.io badge row

  from the kyo-web-online template was intentionally NOT carried over.

  three-exception clause (license preamble + filename block + JSDoc on
  exported helpers documenting non-obvious WHY)
- =.github/SECURITY.org=, =CONTRIBUTING.org=, =.github/workflows/ci.yml=
  — scrubbed =.gga= references
